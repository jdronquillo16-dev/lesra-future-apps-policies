3. Information We Collect

We collect NO personal information whatsoever. No name, email address, phone number, location data, device identifier, or any other personally identifiable information is collected, stored, or transmitted to our systems by this App.

3.1 Local Device Storage Only

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, using Android’s SharedPreferences mechanism. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party through our systems:

  • App settings and preferences (e.g., audio on/off, TTS voice selection, music and sound effects toggles)
  • Lesson progress and completion status
  • Daily learning streak count and best streak record
  • Stars earned and unlocked/equipped rewards (backgrounds and stickers)
  • Optional child display name or profile label, if entered by the parent or user — stored on-device only, never transmitted
  • Whether the first-launch onboarding tutorial has been completed

3.2 What We Do NOT Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location or GPS data
  • No device identifiers (collected by us)
  • No camera or microphone recordings
  • No behavioral profiles or user-tracking data built by us
  • No biometric data of any kind

4. Text-to-Speech (TTS)

The App uses your device’s built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine (provided natively by the Android operating system or installed TTS engines such as Google Text-to-Speech) to pronounce Filipino letters, syllables, words, greetings, and animal sounds. All TTS processing occurs entirely on your device.

We do not make audio recordings, transmit speech data, or receive any output from the device’s TTS engine. The TTS engine is part of the Android platform and is governed by the terms of whoever provided the TTS engine (e.g., Google Text-to-Speech is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy).

5. Device Permissions

The App may request the following Android permission:

  • Internet access — required solely to allow Google AdMob to serve advertisements and for Firebase services (Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config) to function. This permission is not used by us to transmit any personal user data.

The App does NOT request or use the following permissions:

  • Camera or microphone access
  • Location (GPS or network-based)
  • Access to Contacts, Call Logs, or Messages
  • Access to Files, Photos, or External Storage
  • Usage Access or Device Administrator privileges

6. Advertising — Google AdMob

The App may display advertisements served by Google AdMob (a Google LLC service). Advertisements appear only as a global banner at the bottom of the screen and as interstitial ads shown no more than once every five minutes. No native ads are displayed inside game or lesson screens.

Child-Directed Advertising: Because this App is specifically designed for children, we configure AdMob with child-directed treatment (TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT = yes), under-age-of-consent treatment (TAG_FOR_UNDER_AGE_OF_CONSENT = yes), and maximum ad content rating G. This means AdMob will not use behavioral tracking or interest-based advertising profiles when serving ads in this App. All ads are non-personalized and G-rated only, in full compliance with Google’s Families Policy and COPPA.

Interstitial ads are closeable within 5 seconds and are never shown immediately upon app launch, in compliance with Google Play Families Policy requirements.

Relevant Google policies and resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob / Ads Help: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Families Policy: https://play.google.com/about/families/

Parents and guardians may opt out of ad personalization at any time through Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on the device, or by resetting the device’s advertising identifier.

7. Firebase Services

The App integrates the following Google Firebase services, which may collect limited anonymous technical data:

7.1 Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics automatically collects anonymous crash reports and error logs when the App experiences a technical failure. This data includes stack traces, device model, OS version, and app version — but no personally identifiable information. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs to improve App stability.

7.2 Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage events such as which lessons are started or completed, which mini-games are played, and streak milestone achievements. This data is aggregated and anonymous — it does not identify individual users. It is used solely to understand how children engage with the App so we can improve the learning experience.

7.3 Firebase Remote Config

Remote Config allows us to update certain App settings remotely (such as ad frequency and app version information) without requiring a full app update. No personal data is collected through Remote Config.

All Firebase services are configured in compliance with Google’s data processing terms. Firebase data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Optional In-App Subscriptions

The App may offer an optional subscription plan (via Google Play Billing) to remove advertisements. Subscribing is entirely voluntary. If you choose to subscribe:

  • All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play — we are not involved in the payment transaction
  • We do NOT receive, store, or process any payment card information, billing address, or personal financial data
  • We do NOT collect any additional personal information as part of the subscription process
  • Google Play provides us only with an anonymized subscription status token sufficient to unlock the ad-free experience within the App
  • Subscription management (renewal, cancellation, refunds) is handled through the Google Play Store

Google Play’s Terms of Service govern all subscription transactions: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

9. No Cloud Backend

LESRA Future Apps does not operate any proprietary server, cloud database, or backend infrastructure for the Abakada App. There is no transmission of personal user data between the App and our systems at any time.

The App functions as a standalone local application. All learning progress, streak data, star counts, unlocked rewards, and user preferences are stored exclusively on the device via Android SharedPreferences. Internet access is used only by third-party SDKs (AdMob, Firebase) as described in this policy.
