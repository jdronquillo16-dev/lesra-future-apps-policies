2. App Description and Purpose

Abakada – Filipino Literacy for Kids is a free educational mobile application designed to help young children learn the Filipino alphabet (Abakada), letter sounds, syllables, common Filipino words, greetings, cultural terms, and vocabulary through interactive lessons, 8 mini-games, a picture dictionary with animal sounds, word families practice, a finger-trace letter game, and an unlockable rewards system.

The App is intended exclusively for educational and personal, non-commercial use by children under the supervision of a parent or guardian.
