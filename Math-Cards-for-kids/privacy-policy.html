Math-Cards-for-kids

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Math-Cards-for-kids is an educational app for children and families. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when using the app.

2. Data Policy / Data Collection Disclosure

2.1 Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses
  • No precise location data
  • No contacts, photos, videos, microphone, or camera data
  • No payment or financial information

2.2 Data Processed on Device

  • Learning progress (for example: completed card sets, scores, streaks)
  • Basic app settings (for example: sound or difficulty preferences)

This data remains on the user device for core app functionality.

2.3 Third-Party Services

If enabled in a future release, Google Play services or AdMob may process limited technical data under their own policies. We do not sell personal data.

3. Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for child and family use. Parents or guardians should supervise use. If you believe child personal data was submitted, contact us so we can review and address the concern.

4. How Data Is Used

  • Run app features
  • Save educational progress
  • Improve app stability and user experience

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data. Data may be disclosed only when required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

On-device app data remains until users clear app data or uninstall the app. Parents and guardians can remove data at any time through device settings.

7. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, or loss of data. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted in this repository and/or in the app listing.
