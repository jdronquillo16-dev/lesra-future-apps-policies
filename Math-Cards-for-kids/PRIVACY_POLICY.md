## 2. Data Policy / Data Collection Disclosure

We follow a **data minimization** approach.

### 2.1 Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect
- No account registration data
- No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses
- No precise location data
- No contacts, photos, videos, microphone, or camera data
- No payment or financial information

### 2.2 Data Processed on Device
- Learning progress (for example: completed card sets, scores, streaks)
- Basic app settings (for example: sound/music preferences, difficulty settings)

This information is stored locally on the user’s device for core app functionality.

### 2.3 Third-Party Services
If ads or analytics are enabled in a release, related processing may be performed by Google services (such as Google Play or AdMob) under their own policies.  
We do not sell personal data.
