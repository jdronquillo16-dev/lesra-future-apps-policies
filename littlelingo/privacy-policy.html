3. Information We Collect

The App is designed on data minimization principles. We do not gather personal profiles or tracking identifiers from children.

3.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All educational achievements, progress scores, and customization details are stored securely on your local device. This data is kept strictly inside the App's sandbox space (packaged using an offline Room Database) and is never transmitted to us or third-party servers:

  • Earned Game Points (stars, score counts) used to unlock chests
  • Daily Streaks (log counts to foster consistency)
  • Unlocked Sticker Collections & Milestones
  • Completed Tracing exercises and badges
  • App configuration settings (sound preferences, levels)

3.2 Parent-Facing Features & Verification Data

We implement a strict Parental Gate (requiring solving arithmetic challenges) before parents can access standard external features:

  • Google Sign-In: If a parent/guardian chooses to link an account for premium subscription preservation, Google provides an email address, user display name, and standard authentication token. This data is strictly used to authenticate the adult subscriber. It is never used to profile or track the child.
  • Google Play Billing: Premium upgrades are processed safely through Google Play's native billing system. We do not collect or store your billing credentials, credit card numbers, or transaction metadata. All financial details are processed strictly by Google.

3.3 Safe AI & Speech Processing

  • Gemini API Usage: The App uses the Gemini API via a secure backend structure to generate rich, kid-friendly spelling keywords and reading cues. We do not transmit any child's user-specific identifiers, locations, or files to the Gemini server.
  • Voice Synthesis and speech checking: To encourage phonics mastery, the App utilizes Android's built-in Text-To-Speech (TTS) and voice recognition. If microphone inputs are verified for word matching, the audio is processed completely locally or transiently and is never captured, recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.

3.4 Data We Do NOT Collect

We strictly enforce that:

  • We do NOT track geographic location (GPS or network coordinates).
  • We do NOT access the device contact list, photo gallery, or device files.
  • We do NOT use advertising identifiers, behavior trackers, or analytical profiling.

4. Mixed Audience & Advertising

Little Lingo is a general-audience educational application that appeals to both children (5 & under, 6-8, 9-12) and adults. To ensure a safe experience for all users while maintaining the App's availability, we implement the following advertising and safety measures:

🛡️ Age Gate & Ad Compliance

  • Age Gate: When the App first boots up, users are presented with an Age Gate.
  • Child Users (Under 13): If a user indicates they are under 13, the App explicitly tags all ad requests as CHILD (TFCD/TFUA) via the Google TFAT API. This disables personalized advertising and behavioral tracking for these users.
  • Adult Users (13+): Users over 13 may see standard advertisements.
  • Ad-Free Experience: Advertisements are removed for all users who have an active Premium Subscription.

5. How We Use Your Data

Any transient, local, or verified data is strictly used for the following educational and functional purposes:

  • To operate local minigames (Phonics Pals, Word Safari, Storyland, Trace Play).
  • To track milestones and streaks to motivate the child.
  • To manage, process, and respect premium subscription statuses linked to your Google Play account.
  • To deliver dynamic, children-approved answers safely using generative educational AI frameworks.
  • To serve advertisements in compliance with age-based safety requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely only on standard, secure third-party modules that meet rigorous Children & Families guidelines:

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements. For child users, ads are served in a non-personalized, COPPA-compliant manner.
  • Google Play Services: Powers essential app stability, crash logs, and localized features.
  • Google Play Billing & Google Sign-In: Handles premium checkouts and identity verification strictly accessed inside the Parental Gate for adults.
  • Google Gemini API: Generates creative adaptive phonics prompts.

7. Device Permissions

The App requests specific permission scopes strictly when necessary to enable key educational games:

  • INTERNET: Required to query secure educational APIs (Gemini), Google Billing subscription verification, and to serve advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • AD_ID: Used by the Google AdMob SDK for adult users to provide relevant advertising experiences. This is disabled for users identified as children via the Age Gate.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Optional permission utilized solely for real-time educational speech validation. Audio input is analyzed locally, translated transiently, and instantly dismissed. No audio files are stored or transmitted.
