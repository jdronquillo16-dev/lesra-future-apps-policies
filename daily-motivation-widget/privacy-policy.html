Quick Summary

  • Offline-first: all proverbs, favorites, and cached audio are stored locally.
  • Child-safe: Age Gate on first launch; non-personalized ads for children.
  • Optional Google Sign-In for subscription sync only.
  • Subscriptions handled via Google Play Billing; no payment data stored by us.

1. Introduction

LESRA Future Apps ("we", "our", "us") operates Daily Motivation Widget. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information.

2. Offline-first & Local Data

The App stores all user content (proverbs, favorites, reflections, and cached audio) locally in a private SQLite/Room database. We do not maintain external servers for personal content.

3. Children's Privacy & Age Gate

We implement an Age Gate on first launch. Users under 13 are treated as child-directed; AdMob is configured to serve non-personalized contextual ads and TFUA/TFAT is applied. We do not knowingly collect PII from children under 13.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google Analytics/Firebase (anonymized), Google Play Billing (payments), and optional Google Sign-In (subscription sync). These services may collect data per their own policies.

5. User Controls

Restore purchases, revoke Google Sign-In, and clear local data via app settings or by uninstalling the app.

6. Security

We use platform security but cannot guarantee absolute security of stored data.
