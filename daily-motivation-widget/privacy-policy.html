PRIVACY POLICY - DIWA DAILY WISDOM
Last Updated: June 24, 2026

LESRA Future Apps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the "Daily Motivation Widget" mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information and comply with privacy standards, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. 100% OFFLINE-FIRST PHILOSOPHY & LOCAL DATA STORAGE
The App is designed with an offline-first philosophy. 
- All of your learning progress, saved proverbs, favorite sentences, and typography personalization settings are stored 100% locally on your device in a secure private Room SQLite database.
- We do NOT maintain external database servers for your personal usage data, and we do not collect, harvest, or transmit any of your local proverbs or personal notes.

2. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY & COPPA COMPLIANCE (AGE GATE)
The App is a general-audience application that appeals to both children and adults. To protect young users and comply with COPPA and Google Play family guidelines, we implement an mandatory Age Gate upon the first boot of the application:
- If a user specifies an age under 13, the App immediately flags the user as a child.
- For children users, the App explicitly tags all Google AdMob ad requests with the Tag for Child-Directed Treatment (TFUA / TFAT) via the official Google Mobile Ads SDK APIs. This disables personalized behavioral advertising, ensures that only family-safe, non-personalized contextual ads are loaded, and restricts tracking.
- We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13.

3. THIRD-PARTY SDK SERVICES & INTEGRATIONS
To support the application's ongoing development, cross-device synchronization, and secure transaction handling, we integrate the following industry-standard Google services, which may automatically collect certain device metadata:

A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
- Used to serve ads in the free ad-supported tier.
- Ad impressions and ad clicks are tracked automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
- For adult users, personalized ads are delivered. For children users under 13, child-directed (TFUA) compliance is strictly enforced.

B. Google Analytics & Firebase Analytics
- Used to understand app performance, stability, and engagement.
- Tracks basic usage data automatically, including:
  - App first opens and installs.
  - Daily Active Users (DAU) and Monthly Active Users (MAU).
  - Average session lengths and screen view counts.
  - Crash reports and Application Not Responding (ANR) logs.
- All analytics data is anonymized and carries no personal identifiers.

C. Google Play Billing (Google Play In-App Purchases)
- Handles our premium $3.00 USD/month Ad-Free Subscription.
- All transaction processing, payment instrument handling, and refunds are managed entirely and securely by Google Play. We do not have access to your credit card or financial accounts.

D. Google Sign-In
- Allows users to securely sign in using their Google accounts to synchronize active subscription statuses across multiple devices (including mobile phones and Wear OS smartwatches).
- We only access basic account metadata (public profile name and email address) for sync verification purposes. This data is never sold or shared.

4. USER RIGHTS & EXERCISING CONTROL
- Restoring Purchases: If you change devices or re-install the App, you can instantly sync your active ad-free subscription status by tapping the "Restore Purchases" button in the Settings menu.
- Revoking Google Sign-In: You can disconnect your Google Account at any time by tapping the "Log Out" button in Settings.
- Data Deletion: Because your progress is stored entirely locally on your device, you can completely erase all your saved data, learning tracks, and local preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

5. SECURITY OF YOUR DATA
The security of your local data is highly important. We leverage native Android security measures to isolate the App's private SQLite database. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure.
