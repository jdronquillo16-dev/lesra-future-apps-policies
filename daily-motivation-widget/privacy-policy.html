PRIVACY POLICY - Daily Motivation Widget
Last Updated: June 24, 2026

LESRA Future Apps ("we," "our," or "us") operates the "Daily Motivation Widget" mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information and comply with privacy standards, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. OFFLINE-FIRST & LOCAL DATA STORAGE
- The App is designed with an offline-first philosophy. All learning progress, favorites, and personalization settings are stored locally on the device.

2. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY & AGE GATE
- The App implements an Age Gate on first launch. If a user indicates age under 13, we treat the account as child-directed, tag AdMob requests accordingly, and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. For child-directed users we disable personalized advertising and only serve non-personalized contextual ads.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
- Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): used to serve ads; ad impressions and clicks are tracked by the SDK.
- Google Analytics / Firebase: anonymized usage and performance metrics.
- Google Play Billing: handles subscription purchases and receipts.
- Google Sign-In: optional, used only to synchronize subscription state.

4. USER RIGHTS
- Users can restore purchases, revoke Google Sign-In, and delete local data by clearing the app storage or uninstalling.

5. SECURITY
- We leverage platform security to protect local data but cannot guarantee absolute security.
