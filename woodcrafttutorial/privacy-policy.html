1. Introduction

LESRA Future Apps (“we”, “us”, “our”) develops and operates Woodcraft Tutorial (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding the information we collect, use, and share when you use our App.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge and agree to this Privacy Policy. If you are a parent or guardian allowing a child to use the App, you accept this policy on the child’s behalf.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage. This information is used to improve our services and ensure the App functions properly.

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings
  • Usage Data: Features accessed, buttons clicked, screens viewed, and duration of use
  • Log Data: IP address (truncated for privacy), browser type, and timestamps
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics collected via Firebase Analytics
  • Advertising Data: Non-personalized ad interaction data collected by Google AdMob (we do not control this data collection)

2.2 Information You Provide

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Account Information: If you choose to use Google Sign-In, we receive your Google account ID and basic profile information solely for the purpose of restoring purchases across devices
  • Communications: If you contact us via email, we retain the content of your message along with your email address

2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect: names, email addresses (unless provided via contact), phone numbers, physical addresses, payment information, or any other personally identifiable information beyond what is described above.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our App and services
  • To understand how users interact with our App and enhance user experience
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues
  • To send you important updates about the App (if you have opted in)
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted third-party services that help us operate our business:
    • Google Firebase Analytics: For anonymous usage analytics
    • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements (with child-directed content settings)
    • Google Sign-In: For optional authentication to restore purchases across devices
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Specifically:

  • Analytics data is retained for a maximum of 2 years
  • Account information (if applicable) is retained until account deletion
  • Communications with our support team are retained for 3 years

7. Children's Privacy

Woodcraft Tutorial is designed to be family-friendly. We take special care to protect children's privacy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age
  • The App does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information to use core features
  • An age gate is presented on first launch to ensure age-appropriate content
  • When we detect that a user may be under 13, we limit data collection to only what is strictly necessary for app functionality
  • We do not serve behavioral advertising to users we know or suspect are under 13
  • If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete such information promptly
